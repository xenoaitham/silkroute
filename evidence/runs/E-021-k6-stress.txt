3. Infra integrity never broke at any rung (zero 5xx/timeouts/resets across ~43.9k requests across segments A+B+warm-110):

   p95/p99-vs-error-rate shape of the breaking-point rule and of alert SLO-1.
4. All numbers: shared contended host (other stacks running), sim mode, k6 local. Absolute values will differ
   on idle/dedicated hardware; the SHAPE (hold to ~90-110, cold-start cliff, stock ceiling) is the transferable result.
