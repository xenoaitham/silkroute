## 2. Fixes needed (for the orchestrator to apply; pack files untouched by SEC-1)

### Fix 1 — REQUIRED: replace the PDPA s. 26 quotation in `docs/interview/qa-deep-drill-40.md` Q26 (line 188)

The sentence in quotation marks is not the statutory text of s. 26 in any version (2012 original or current). Replace with the current statutory wording and attribute the operational steps to the implementing Regulations:

> Section 26 is the Transfer Limitation Obligation: "An organisation must not transfer any personal data to a country or territory outside Singapore except in accordance with requirements prescribed under this Act to ensure that organisations provide a standard of protection to personal data so transferred that is comparable to the protection under this Act." The prescribed requirements live in the Personal Data Protection Regulations 2021, reg. 10: before transferring, "take appropriate steps to ascertain whether, and to ensure that, the recipient … is bound by legally enforceable obligations … to provide to the transferred personal data a standard of protection that is at least comparable to the protection under the Act."

The existing follow-on sentence in Q26 ("In practice that means binding the recipient — contractual clauses, intra-group agreements — to a comparable standard…") stays as is — it matches reg. 10/11.

### Fix 2 — REQUIRED: correct the citation-log row for PDPA in the same file (line 293)

Replace the verbatim-anchor cell and delete the false parenthetical. Suggested replacement anchor text:

> Current s. 26(1) (Singapore Statutes Online, as at 23 Jul 2026): "An organisation must not transfer any personal data to a country or territory outside Singapore except in accordance with requirements prescribed under this Act to ensure that organisations provide a standard of protection to personal data so transferred that is comparable to the protection under this Act." Operational steps: PDP Regulations 2021 reg. 10(1) ("take appropriate steps … legally enforceable obligations … at least comparable"). Note: s. 26 has used the "except in accordance with requirements prescribed" structure since 2012 (originally "shall"; "must" in the current Revised Edition); the 2012 text never read "is satisfied" (that phrase is in ss. 22/29), and "has taken appropriate steps" is regulation language, not s. 26 text.

Keep the SSO URL; it is correct. Optionally add the Regulations URL: https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/PDPA2012-S63-2021 (reg. 10).

### Fix 3 — OPTIONAL (nuance hardening for Q25): Art. 38 "one of three mechanisms"

The statute enumerates a fourth catch-all item ("meeting other conditions set forth by laws and administrative regulations and by the national cyberspace department") plus a treaty-equivalence closing paragraph. "One of three mechanisms" is the standard practitioner framing and is defensible, but if the interviewer knows PIPL, "one of the following" is safer:

> "…must meet one of the routes in Article 38 — in practice three compliance pathways: the CAC security assessment (Article 40 route), certification by a professional institution, or the CAC standard contract filed with the provincial cyberspace authority (the filing duty sits in the CAC's 2023 Standard Contract Measures, not in PIPL's own text), with Article 38 also recognising other conditions set by law or the CAC and treaty-based equivalence."

Also applicable to the whiteboard board line (line 61): consider "Art. 38: security assessment / standard contract / certification (+ other conditions by law/CAC)".

### Fix 4 — OPTIONAL (whiteboard line 63): "ICP filing applies only to mainland-hosted public services"

Tighten to match the source verbatim: "ICP filing applies only to services hosted on servers in the Chinese mainland" (Alibaba Cloud's words). "Public" is the board shorthand's own addition; Q28's detailed version is already correct.

### Fix 5 — OPTIONAL (residency shorthands: qa Q2 line 32, resume line 43, whiteboard line 15)

These restate scenario constraint C1 and are fine as scenario framing, but each flattens PIPL's actual rule. Lowest-friction hardening: prefix with "scenario C1" or "PIPL-driven", e.g. whiteboard line 15: "Chinese PII stays in China under scenario C1 (PIPL-driven residency)" — leaving the accurate mechanics to Q25/Q29 and the 18:30–21:00 section, which already state them correctly.

### Fix 6 — OPTIONAL (qa Q28 line 202): add the process URL

The two cited Alibaba URLs verify the quotes but not the "provider pre-review then MIIT-side review" sentence. Add: https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/icp-filing/basic-icp-service/user-guide/icp-filing-application-overview (Step 4: "Communications administration review").
